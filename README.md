### CHEM-000549: Untreated-seawater electrolysis without chlorine or precipitation
- **Subfield:** Water splitting, hydrogen production and fuel cells
- **Open:** Operate complete electrolysers on variable natural seawater while suppressing chlorine chemistry, calcium/magnesium precipitation and corrosion with little conditioning.
- **Status:** A longstanding paired-electrode challenge. Separate 2026 advances include 1,500-hour simulated-seawater anode tests and natural-seawater cathode antifouling; the full integrated target remains open.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** It would establish whether direct saline-water use offers a practical system advantage.
